# Stage: intent

Say, in one or two sentences, what outcome this change is reaching for from the
point of view of the person who uses the product. Later stages review the code
against your answer, so be specific about the behavior, not the files.

- Start with "The author is working towards".
- Describe the outcome, not the mechanics: not "adds a flag and a helper" but what
  becomes possible, fixed or safer for a user.
- Trust the diff over the description when they disagree, and say what the diff
  actually does.
- Read repository files when you need to know what the product does around the
  change; do not guess.
- If the purpose cannot be told, write "Unclear:" followed by what is missing.

Reply with those sentences only: plain text, no list, no heading, no findings.
